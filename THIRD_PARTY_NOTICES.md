# Third-party notices

## Challenge assets

`public/assets/` is supplied by the [Jungle Gaming game developer challenge](https://github.com/junglegaming/game-developer-challenge). The original archive and repository did not include a standalone license file. These files are retained as the visual/audio materials required for the challenge submission; no blanket license for their independent redistribution is asserted here.

The ship art follows Kenney's [Pirate Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/pirate-pack), published under CC0. The challenge also contains custom UI, references, branding and sounds; the Kenney license should not be assumed to cover those additions. No additional stock artwork is used. The nautical chart, island geometry, water and interface styling are drawn in CSS/Pixi.
